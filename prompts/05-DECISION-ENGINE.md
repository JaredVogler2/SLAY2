# PROMPT 05 — SLAY2 EXPLAINABLE, LEGAL CHOICES, MATH AND LONG-HORIZON OPTIMIZATION

Build the core recommendation engine with version-pinned factual rules and actual fair search/model evidence from previous phases when available. The user needs actionable immediate choice and alternative reasoning, not merely a tier-list or unverifiable single-number "win %" estimate.

## Universal contract
`POST /api/v1/recommend`: `gameId: STS2, build, channel, character, A0-A10, gameMode, partyState?, act/biome/floor, unlockProgress?, visibleState, decisionType, offeredLegalActions, uncertaintyState?, riskPreference, budget, objective: Act3Clear`. Verify actual game constraints, validate via typed schemas. Response: `recommendation, alternatives, legalActions, explanation, tradeoffs, exactOrEmpiricalOrCalibratedOrHeuristic, probabilityEstimate? interval? n? assumptions, supportedMechanics, missingInputs, stateDigest, gameRuleVersion, policyVersion, latency`. Refuse invalid or unsupported, don't invent model probabilities.

## Choice services
1. **Offered card draft** (3 options + skip, special rewards, upgraded/enchant/pool changes, reroll/sacrifice): evaluate specific current deck, role mix, act/boss, generated cards, energy, draw/cycle, synergy, ascension, upcoming biome, relics, debuffs, scaling, opportunity cost, probabilistic card draw. Explain conditions under which ranking changes and why skip beats picks in narrow cases. No omniscient future draw data.
2. **Ancient boon selection**: identify actually encountered Ancient and its offered legal choices, relic restrictions/unlock, current build, act transition heal and Ascension2 modifier, stateful relic/item effects. Evaluate immediate power and end-game survivability; no STS1 boss-relic assumption.
3. **Map Pathfinder**: interactive known map DAG including Act1 biome-specific monsters/boss; legal adjacent steps; evaluate elite rewards vs attrition and upgrade/card/relic/shop path; stochastic event nodes with conditional rates and viable rest options; explain high-risk vs safe routes. Do not project missing next act as known.
4. **Combat Tactics**: current hand/energy/enemy intents/potion/relic/character state; legally enumerate attack/block/card/target/created choice/endturn, own summons and ally targets if supported, sequence and triggered effects. Exact immediate damage and resource deltas, bounded hidden-state battle rollouts. Show lethal plan and risk distribution, and recompute after unknown draws/random effects. Evaluate slow scaling versus instant damage, potion savings and next-turn resilience.
5. **Probabilistic HP-loss and targeting laboratory**: original Sim the Spire-inspired combinatorial PMF; exact small state, Monte Carlo with declared samples for complex state; 2D enemy HP threshold / current hand conditional optimal target regions, alternative actions and CVaR/death risk. No copied charts, explicit search approximation error.
6. **Merchant optimizer**: budgeted joint buying cards/relics/potions and removal, special merchant, discounts, inventory conditional exclusions, changing removal prices at A6, shared vs personal constraints for co-op; compare buy/save and opportunity cost.
7. **Rest sites**: rest, smith, and STS2 unlock/relic-specific options (Mend/Dig/Train/Kindle/Cook/Hatch/Clone only when actually available), A2 Ancient healing differences not accidentally applied to campfire, incoming boss, deck upgrade opportunity and current HP. All heal math exact and source-linked.
8. **Potions**: exact inventory, target, usage order/availability, combat HP saved against carry value/slot, opponent intent and current hidden potion pity counter belief, forced-drop relics; no unavailable potion recommendation.
9. **Events & unknown nodes**: compare actually presented valid choices, conditional risks/penalties/enchantments/afflictions/transform, no oracle event contents; unknown node distribution from observed counter history and eligibility flags with uncertainty.
10. **Enchantment and card transformations**: target legality, one-enchantment limits and stacking amount (per game version), positive/negative afflictions, created card and character-specific costs.
11. **Run health/deck audit**: consistency of draw/energy/block, scaling for known Act2 Hive / Act3 Glory bosses, encounter matchup, curse/affliction risk, Ancient build strategy, synergy graph; contextual recommendations vs static rankings.
12. **Co-op coordinated advice — gated**: if tested, legal teammate-help card/potion/target strategy, shared event choices/Ancient and map votes, turn sequencing and surviving as a team, no misleading independent solo evaluation. Otherwise mark co-op not yet supported.

## Correct probability mathematics
Versioned stateful rare offset from -5 to +40, sequence per reward choice, A7 Scarcity and pack uniqueness; unknown room conditional fight/chest/shop checks, potion initial40 +/-10 and elite bonus, C65/U25/R10 by source, nonrare upgrade act chance, Ancient/unlock conditional pooling, exact draw hypergeometric only under assumptions, combat outgoing/incoming HP loss conditional. All probabilities must cite rules version and be checked via pure calculator tests. Do not report "83% next rare" based on nominal base chance while rare pity unknown. For unknown counter, derive posterior over possible counter states and interval, not infer exact from current floor.

## Explanation and reliability
Default output: Recommended [action], reasons grounded in *this* deck/act/current hand; two alternatives and what circumstances make them better; quantitative tradeoffs with sample n, interval and method; confidence that differentiates exact formula / sampled combat / calibrated network / heuristics. Provide how-calculated expandable pane, evidence source, patch freshness. No fake salience or LLM explanation of learned model weights as causal influence. Other player data only as observed association.

## Acceptance
Test >=100 reference scenarios across all supported types and special condition/synergy variants; legal-only action property, risk/source conditioning, fast approximate fallback, OOD/unsupported branch. Manual mode can return good actionable advice with 3 offered cards and a few other inputs; advanced details optional. Commit tested providers and API fixtures. Proceed phase 06.
